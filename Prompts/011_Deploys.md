# Deployment Text Rules

* Deployment Ready: Monday to Thursday between 0600 and 1630
* Avoid Deployment:
	* Weekdays except friday: 1630 to 2030 and 0400 to 0600
        * Friday 0400 to 1200
* Do Not Deploy:
	* Friday after 1200
        * Weekends
        * Weekdays between 2030 and next day's 0400
